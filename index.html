Frequently Asked

Questions from our
customers

Do you supply glass bottles and jars for the South African market?

Yes. Alliance Glass Pack's South Africa operations are based in Killarney Gardens, Cape Town, supplying glass bottles, jars, and custom packaging locally with stock held on-site for fast turnaround.

What types of glass bottles do you offer?

Our range includes wine bottles, beer bottles, champagne bottles, glass water bottles, juice bottle glass, perfume oil bottles, dropper bottles, and reed diffuser bottles - across both stock items and full customisation.

Do you supply mason jars in South Africa?

Yes, mason jars and general glass containers are part of our Food & Beverage packaging range, alongside jars for preserving, honey, sauces, and spreads.

Can you supply dropper bottles for serums and fragrance oils?

Yes. We supply dropper bottles and glass containers suited to serums, essential oils, and fragrance oil South Africa brands as part of our Beauty & Personal Care range.

Do you make reed diffuser bottles?

Yes, reed diffuser bottles are part of our Beauty & Personal Care packaging range, supplied both as stock items and through custom development.

Can you develop a custom glass bottle shape that isn't in your current stock range?

Yes. Our New Product Development service covers concept, design, prototyping, and full-scale manufacturing, working directly with mould manufacturers - so custom shapes outside our current stock range can be developed for your brand.

Where is Alliance Glass Pack based, and do you deliver across South Africa?

Our South African facility is a 2,500m² warehouse in Killarney Gardens, Cape Town, with a dedicated local team supporting fast delivery across South Africa, backed by a global network of five warehouses across two continents.
