Jars, bottles, and glass containers for oils, sauces, honey, spreads, condiments, and juice bottle glass. This includes mason jars for preserving and retail, cold press oil bottles for cold-pressed oils and dressings, and juice bottle glass in a range of sizes - engineered for freshness, designed for the shelf.

Spirits, Wine, Beer & Champagne Packaging

Premium glass for whisky, gin, vodka, wine, beer, and champagne. Shapes and finishes that command attention at point of sale.

Serums, perfume oil bottles, dropper bottles, reed diffusers, cosmetic vials, and skincare containers. Packaging that is as luxurious as the product inside.

Product Range

Every shape,
every category

A closer look at what's included within each of our three market categories — from everyday stock items to fully bespoke packaging development.

01

Food & Beverages

  • Mason jars — for preserving, retail packaging, honey, jams, and artisanal food brands
  • Cold press oil bottles — for cold-pressed olive, avocado, and specialty oils, and salad dressings
  • Juice bottle glass — a range of sizes suited to cold-pressed juice, smoothies, and functional beverages
  • Sauce & condiment jars — for chutneys, relishes, spreads, and table sauces
  • General glass containers — for spices, dry goods, and retail food packaging
02

Spirits, Wine, Beer & Champagne

  • Wine bottles — Bordeaux, Burgundy, and custom silhouettes for still and fortified wines
  • Beer bottles — standard and custom-embossed formats for craft and commercial breweries
  • Champagne & sparkling bottles — pressure-rated glass built for carbonation
  • Spirits bottles — whisky, gin, vodka, and craft spirit formats with premium finishes
  • Glass water bottles — still and sparkling water formats for hospitality and retail
03

Beauty & Personal Care

  • Dropper bottles — for serums, essential oils, and fragrance oil brands across South Africa
  • Perfume oil bottles — compact, premium formats for fragrance and attar oils
  • Reed diffuser bottles — stock and custom shapes for the home fragrance category
  • Cosmetic jars & vials — for creams, balms, and skincare formulations
  • Spray & pump bottles — for toners, mists, and liquid cosmetic products
