How It Works

From enquiry
to delivery

Whether you're ordering from stock or developing something entirely new, here's what working with us looks like.

01

Enquiry & Consultation

Tell us your volumes, timelines, and whether you need a stock item or something custom. We'll recommend the right starting point.

02

Design & Prototyping

For custom shapes, our team works directly with mould manufacturers on concept sketches, technical drawings, and physical samples before tooling is cut.

03

Manufacturing & QC

Production runs to agreed specifications and AQL (Acceptable Quality Level) standards, with industry-standard tolerances applied throughout.

04

Delivery

Stock items ship fast from our Cape Town warehouse; custom orders are scheduled against your production timeline, with shipment tracked from origin to door.

Quality Standards

Consistency you
can specify

Every order — whether it's a stock item or a fully custom development — is produced against agreed specifications and industry-standard AQL (Acceptable Quality Level) tolerances.

We work to the same quality disciplines regardless of order size: dimensional accuracy, wall thickness consistency, and finish quality are checked before goods leave our facility.

For brands filling, capping, or labelling our glass themselves, we're happy to share technical drawings and dimensional specifications ahead of production to confirm compatibility with your line.
