Online privacy is extremely crucial for any organization and Alliance Glass Pack PTY LTD is committed to ensure and safeguard the information provided by various visitors visiting our Web site. As an organization manufactures and sells glass packaging, Alliance Glass Pack PTY LTD, gathers information from site users and visitors essential to providing the best experience possible, and at the same time Alliance Glass Pack PTY LTD is dedicated to ensuring and implement the concept of privacy. Our Privacy Policy assures safeguarding the interests and information of visitors to its website.

Loggers to Alliance Glass Pack PTY LTD website are requested to get themselves familiarized with the Privacy Policy of Alliance Glass Pack PTY LTD. When any visitor is providing any personal data, the visitor is agreeing to the terms and conditions stipulated under our Privacy Policy and is bound by the same. In case of not agreeing to our privacy policy, the visitor/logger is requested to discontinue the visit.

Our Privacy Policy covers the following:

Users subscribing to information or posting a service request at Alliance Glass Pack PTY LTD are requested to provide some personal information specifically identifiable data, which includes email id and Phone number. The said information becomes the property of Alliance Glass Pack PTY LTD and is shared, distributed or disclosed to third parties in accordance with the Privacy Policy only. However, registration is required for visitors who require specific services. Otherwise, visitors are free to visit us without getting registered. Alliance Glass Pack PTY LTD shall not sell or rent such personal information provided to it by the visitor/logger.

The information may include one or more of the following:

Contact Number Information: The collection of the said data is for the purpose of identity and for providing the best possible assistance from our end and it only helps the Visitor/Logger in the long run of association with Alliance Glass Pack PTY LTD.

E-mail information: When Alliance Glass Pack PTY LTD receives e-mails from visitors, it retains the content of any e-mail messages sent as well as details of such e-mail address.

GST and PAN Number: The collection of the said data is for the purpose of enable smooth business transactions for the Visitor/Logger in the long run of association with Alliance Glass Pack PTY LTD.

We will use your information to respond to you, regarding the reason you contacted us. We will not share your information with any third party outside of our organization, other than as necessary to fulfill your request or mandated by the laws of the land.

Security

We take precautions to protect your information. When you submit sensitive information via the website, your information is protected both online and offline. Wherever we collect sensitive information (such as credit card data or any financial data), that information is encrypted and transmitted to us in a secure way. You can verify this by looking for a closed lock icon at the bottom of your web browser, or looking for "https" at the beginning of the address of the web page.

While we use encryption to protect sensitive information transmitted online, we also protect your information offline. The computers/servers in which we store personally identifiable information are kept in a secure environment.

Links

This web site contains links to other sites. Please be aware that we are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of such other sites. Third party websites which are accessible from our Site via click-throughs, links or banner advertising may be using Cookies and it is pertinent to mention that Alliance Glass Pack PTY LTD has no access or control over such Cookies and thus do not accept responsibility regarding such cookies. We encourage our users to be aware when they leave our site and to read the privacy statements of any other site that collects personally identifiable information.

Updates

Our Privacy Policy may change from time to time and all updates will be posted on this page.

For any further queries regarding the privacy policy, practices of the site or any other issue, please contact nitin@glasspack.co.
